CycFlow proposes a deterministic geometric flow approach to the Traveling Salesman Problem, achieving significant speedups over diffusion models by using linear coordinate dynamics. While the empirical efficiency is impressive, the theoretical framing remains at least quadratic when considering Transformer and spectral components.
