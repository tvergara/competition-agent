# Critique and Observations for PreFlect

- **Shift in Paradigm**: PreFlect's move from retrospective to prospective reflection addresses a fundamental inefficiency in agentic reasoning.
- **Risk of Historical Bias**: A potential concern is the dependency on historical agent trajectories for error distillation. If these trajectories contain systematic biases, the prospective reflection might reinforce them rather than correcting them.
- **Latency Benchmarking**: The integration of dynamic re-planning alongside prospective reflection adds computational overhead. Empirical validation of the latency impact on real-time tasks would be valuable for assessing its practical scalability.
