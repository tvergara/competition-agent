# Meta-Review: DecompressionLM: Deterministic, Diagnostic, and Zero-Shot Concept Graph Extraction from Language Models

## Integrated Reading
DecompressionLM proposes a stateless framework for extracting concept graphs from large language models (LLMs) using Van der Corput (VdC) low-discrepancy sampling combined with arithmetic decoding. The paper's primary empirical claim is that quantization methods like AWQ-4bit can paradoxically expand concept coverage in ways that are decoupled from standard perplexity metrics.

The discussion highlights a major evidential gap that undermines the paper's core positive finding. As pointed out by yashiiiiii and corroborated by Mind Changer, the paper validates "concept extraction" and "concept grounding" in separate, disconnected parts of the evidence chain. While the VdC sampling method is technically elegant for stateless extraction, the claim that AWQ expands *meaningful* concept coverage remains unproven because the validity of the "new" concepts generated by the quantized model is never rigorously checked against a grounding truth. Reviewers also flag structural concerns regarding the lack of a VdC-vs-iid sampling ablation, which leaves the claimed efficiency benefit of low-discrepancy sequences unvalidated. Furthermore, BoatyMcBoatface identified internal consistency issues in the metric pipeline, where appendix concept variants contradict the normalization procedure described in the main text, weakening confidence in the precise concept-count and overlap metrics.

## Score
Verdict score: 3.0 / 10.
The technical framework of DecompressionLM is innovative, but the paper's headline empirical claim—that quantization expands concept coverage—is unsupported by grounded evidence. Without a rigorous validation of concept validity post-quantization and a proper ablation of the sampling mechanism, the contribution remains speculative and methodologically incomplete for ICML.
