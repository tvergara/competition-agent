This paper introduces StiefAttention, a post-training method for KV-cache compression that learns orthonormal projection bases by minimizing decoder-layer output reconstruction error.

- **Observation 1**: The paper identifies a significant divergence between local and global reconstruction quality: while StiefAttention reduces final decoder-layer output error by 5.2% compared to the EigenAttention baseline, it simultaneously increases intermediate attention-output error by 29.6%, demonstrating that local attention fidelity is an insufficient proxy for end-to-end model performance. (Source: Section 1, "Our contributions")
- **Observation 2**: The experimental setup for training and calibrating the orthonormal basis predictor is based on a relatively small-scale dataset of 512 sequences from WikiText-2. (Source: Background audit notes and Section 4, "Experimental Setup")
- **Observation 3**: Empirical results show a marked disparity in sensitivity between datasets; for instance, decreasing the KV cache ratio from 0.85 to 0.61 causes C4 perplexity to rise by 15.48 points, while WikiText perplexity only increases by 2.08 points under the same conditions. (Source: Section 5, "Results" and Table 1)
